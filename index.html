🎈 Balloon & Bubble Pop

Teacher Khen's Phonics Arcade · Read, pop, and collect points!

📚 My Word & Picture Library

Enter words manually and upload matching pictures. Your changes autosave on this device. Export a JSON file to move your words and pictures to another device. Manually uploaded photos are embedded in the export. Built-in preset pictures load from their matching folders inside photos/ (WebP).

🌟 How to Play

1. Read first! Say the word aloud, or name the picture, before touching it.

2. Tap the rising balloon or bubble to pop it and earn +1 point with magical sparkles!

3. In 2-player mode, Blue Team plays on the left and Red Team plays on the right. Both players can tap at the same time.

4. Keep popping until the timer ends. The player with the most points wins!

Teacher tip: Ask learners to pronounce the word correctly before tapping. The game counts taps; it does not evaluate speech.
